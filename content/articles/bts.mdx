## BTS & ARMY

For thirteen years, BTS and ARMY have shared a story that extends far beyond music. What began in 2013 with seven young artists became a global community built on trust, loyalty, and belonging.

Through every album, every performance, every challenge, and every milestone, millions of people found comfort, inspiration, and connection in the journey of BTS. Their fans, known as ARMY, became more than supporters. They became part of the story.

Together they created one of the most remarkable relationships in modern culture, a bond that crossed languages, borders, and generations. Thirteen years later, the connection remains as strong as ever. Not because of records. Not because of awards. But because people felt seen.

> And in a world searching for connection, that may be BTS's greatest achievement.
