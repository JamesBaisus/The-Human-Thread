## From Nadine, To The Fans

Through a warm video message, Nadine Nassib Njeim spoke directly to the fans who have stood beside her for years. As Momken reaches its final episode, Nadine did not only speak about the end of a series. She spoke about a journey shared with her audience — a bond built through love, excitement, success, sadness, and unforgettable moments.

For more than fifteen years, her fans have been part of her story. They celebrated her victories, supported her through difficult chapters, and remained present through every season of her life and career. In her message, Nadine chose to thank them in a way that feels personal — not with many words, but with the promise of something beautiful coming soon.

> A chapter may be closing with Momken, but between Nadine and her fans, the story continues.
