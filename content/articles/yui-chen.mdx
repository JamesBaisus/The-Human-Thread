In an exclusive interview with The Human Thread_AE, Yui Chen reflects on childhood imagination, memories of her parents and finding expression through fashion.

![](/articles/yui-chen/01.jpg)

---

Yui Chen remembers going out with her parents as a child and feeling that the trees were waving to her. That simple experience of finding imagination in everyday life is something she hopes others will discover through her designs.

Her work also comes from a personal place. When she first began creating this space, it was a way to hold the memories between herself and her parents. Knowing that everyone goes through difficult moments, she hopes others can use it to hold whatever they treasure, or simply escape from reality for a while.

![](/articles/yui-chen/02.jpg)

---

Finding her medium took exploration. When Yui Chen first began making art, she was unsure which form of expression felt right. Through fashion, she discovered how the body and emotion could bring her creations to life.

![](/articles/yui-chen/03.jpg)

---

She hopes people find different meanings in her work. The response she most wants to hear begins with, “Your work reminds me of…” She is curious about the memories and feelings each person brings to it, allowing their own experiences to shape what they see.

Above all, Yui Chen hopes her work encourages people to keep imagining and remain curious like a child. She wants everyone to discover who they are, develop their own way of seeing things and allow themselves to go beyond the limits they have set for themselves.
