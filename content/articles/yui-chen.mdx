## Yui Chen: Childhood, Still in the Making

![](/articles/yui-chen/01.jpg)

---
In conversation with The Human Thread_AE. Yui Chen’s answers have been lightly edited for clarity.

![](/articles/yui-chen/02.jpg)

---
I still remember going out with my parents when I was a child. Whenever I saw the trees, I felt they were all waving to me. That simple feeling of finding imagination in everyday life is what I hope people experience through my designs.

![](/articles/yui-chen/03.jpg)

---
I think we all go through moments in life that feel a little tough. When I first began creating this space, it was a way to hold the memories between me and my parents. I hope other people can also use it to hold whatever they treasure, or simply escape from reality for a while.

![](/articles/yui-chen/04.jpg)

---
When I first explored art, I wasn’t sure which medium felt right for me. Through fashion, I discovered that the body and emotion were the best way to express my work. They bring my creations to life.

![](/articles/yui-chen/05.jpg)

---
I hope different people find different meanings in what I create. What I would most like to hear is, “Your work reminds me of…” I’m curious about the memories and feelings people bring to it.

Above all, I hope my work reminds people to keep imagining and stay curious like a child. I hope everyone can discover who they are and develop their own way of seeing things, allowing themselves to go beyond the limits they have set for themselves.
