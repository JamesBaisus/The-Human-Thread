For more than four decades, Elie Saab has built a language of glamour defined by craftsmanship, femininity and unforgettable red carpet moments. Now that world is stepping into more accessible territory.

The Lebanese couturier is collaborating with H&M on a 55 piece collection spanning eveningwear, tailoring, knitwear and accessories, translating signatures such as lace, embroidery, sequins and rich fabrics into a high street collection. The collaboration is scheduled to launch on 22 October 2026, in selected H&M stores worldwide and online.

![Leather and volume, cut with couture restraint.|right](/edit/elie-saab-hm/01.jpg)

---

It is an unexpected meeting of two fashion worlds: the exclusivity and fantasy associated with Saab, and the global reach of H&M.
