Sometimes the most powerful dreams are expressed in the fewest words.

Selena Gomez recently shared a tender image holding a newborn, captioned simply, "one day..".

![The photograph, and the two words beneath it.|right](/edit/selena-gomez/01.jpg)

---

The moment carries deeper meaning. Selena Gomez has previously spoken openly about learning that carrying a pregnancy herself could pose serious health risks, and about coming to terms with a future that may include surrogacy or adoption.

Her hope, however, remains beautifully simple: to become a mother and build a family of her own.

> Motherhood does not have one path, and sometimes a different journey can lead to the same love.
