A rare celestial moment is unfolding above us.

On 12 August 2026, the Moon passes between Earth and the Sun, creating a total solar eclipse across parts of Greenland, Iceland and Spain, while much of Europe witnesses a dramatic partial eclipse.

![Totality over the water, and everyone who came to watch.|wide](/edit/solar-eclipse/01.jpg)

---

But the next great spectacle is already written in the sky. On 2 August 2027, another total solar eclipse will sweep across southern Spain and North Africa, including Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, Libya and Egypt.

![The last of the light.](/edit/solar-eclipse/02.jpg)
![A crescent sun, burning orange.](/edit/solar-eclipse/03.jpg)

---

For centuries, eclipses have made humanity stop and look upward. We may understand the science now, but perhaps the magic remains the same.

> For a moment, the Sun disappears. And the world remembers to look up.
