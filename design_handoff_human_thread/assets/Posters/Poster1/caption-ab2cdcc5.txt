From the Heart to the Hands

Dolce&Gabbana’s exhibition is more than a celebration of fashion; it is a journey into craftsmanship, memory, and Italian beauty.

From the Heart to the Hands brings together Alta Moda, Alta Sartoria, and Alta Gioielleria, revealing the invisible work behind every creation the embroidery, the devotion, the patience, and the human touch.

Curated by Florence Müller, the exhibition celebrates the artistic legacy of Domenico Dolce and Stefano Gabbana, connecting fashion with architecture, opera, cinema, photography, and the spirit of la dolce vita.

Each room feels like a dialogue between dream and detail, showing that true luxury is not only what we see, but what has been made slowly, carefully, and with soul.
