WHERE COUTURE MEETS ENERGY

Iris van Herpen’s Sonic Starquakes Haute Couture collection moves beyond the traditional language of fashion, entering a space where science, light, and the body become one.

One of the most striking looks, The Fractal Universe, transforms the dress into more than a garment. Charged through scientific process and marked by branching flashes of lightning, the piece carries the feeling of a living surface fragile, powerful, and almost cosmic.

Through this work, couture becomes an experiment in matter and imagination. It reminds us that fashion is not only about beauty or craftsmanship; it can also become a dialogue between nature, technology, and human presence.

In Iris van Herpen’s world, the body does not simply wear the dress. It becomes part of the universe it creates.
