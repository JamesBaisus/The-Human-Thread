SCHIAPARELLI: INTO THE ABYSS
Fall/Winter 2026–2027 Haute Couture
By @danielroseberry

Presented in Paris during Haute Couture Week, The Abyss transforms couture into a language of darkness, desire, and reinvention. Sculpted bodies, surreal textures, and impossible silhouettes remind us that beauty does not always have to be soft. Sometimes, it must unsettle us first.
