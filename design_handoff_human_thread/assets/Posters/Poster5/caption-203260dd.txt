THE TREE THAT HOLDS THE MIST

In Socotra, Yemen, the dragon’s blood tree stands like a sculpture shaped by survival.
Its crown opens like an umbrella, not for decoration, but to collect the mist and protect the earth beneath it.
When its bark is wounded, it releases a deep red resin the reason behind its mythical name.
For centuries, this tree has carried the story of nature’s intelligence, beauty, and endurance.
It reminds us that rarity is not always loud.
Sometimes, it grows slowly, survives quietly, and becomes unforgettable.
